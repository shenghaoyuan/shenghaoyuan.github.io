About me

I am Shenghao Yuan, a researcher at Zhejiang University. My research focuses on formal verification of compilers, operating systems, and virtual machines.

I work at the State Key Laboratory of Blockchain and Data Security and the Hangzhou High-Tech Zone (Binjiang) Institute of Blockchain and Data Security. I am particularly interested in eBPF-related verification and Rust contract specification.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Université de Rennes / Inria-Rennes in 2023, advised by Jean-Pierre Talpin and Frédéric Besson.
